Quantum Time's cover was designed by my son, Jason Simpson. He incorporated many secrets in the cover. Let's examine some of them.


[![](/weebly/qt-cover-full.jpg)](/weebly/qt-cover-full.jpg)


On the front (the right side of the image), you see a futuristic hourglass, filled with mushroom clouds for sand. The clock at the bottom is set for 6:28, which is the date the novel starts and is also my birthday. The clock also doubles as a ring with a Jewel on top. The Jewel is transforming into a bird, like a Phoenix. The ring also looks like a possible engagement ring. Notice how the image starts to distort as it goes to the right... 

How do you see these images relating to the book? 

On the back, some of you noticed the red letters. What does it spell? 

I think the image turned out fantastic! Thank you Jason for your work on this cover!
