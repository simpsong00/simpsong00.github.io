One of the features of my books are Spotify playlists! Each book has a playlist with one song per chapter. The song title or a piece of a lyric may be embedded in the chapter, or there is another, less obvious, connection between the chapter and the song. I thought I'd share all of the playlists together in one place. Here they are! (including the playlist for Quotient, The Game!)

The *Quantum Launch* playlist isn't final yet, but it has a good start! It will be finished before *Quantum Launch* is published!

Enjoy,
Greg
