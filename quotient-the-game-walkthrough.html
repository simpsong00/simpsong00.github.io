Your mission

Two goals run in parallel: collect treasures and solve puzzles to raise your rank, then help Professor Cassandra Eaton complete her time experiment. The main mission asks you to reboot TARDIS, recover the Kyber crystal, build a light saber, reveal the laser clue, power Cassie's quantum console, and follow the Lost Lodge, Lancelot, and Aslan clues to their conclusion.

  • Reboot TARDIS
  • Recover the Kyber crystal
  • Build a light saber
  • Activate the hidden laser
  • Stabilize Cassie's experiment
  • Complete the time-travel sequence

There are 22 treasures worth 2 points each. The mission score can reach 107; completing the ending doubles the possible total to 214.

01 — ENTRYGet into Quotient Headquarters

Begin at the Security Checkpoint. Open the mailbox and take its leaflet, then follow the lane toward headquarters. The corn maze is available to explore but is not required immediately.

Hint 1
Check the mailbox before leaving. Once inside the grounds, explore the kitchen and keep portable supplies.
Hint 2
The kitchen has Rediwhip, bread, and a paring knife. Rediwhip counts as a treasure. Continue around the grounds toward the Fire Pit.
Full Solution
OPEN MAILBOX
TAKE LEAFLET
WEST

At headquarters, collect useful kitchen items. At the Fire Pit, take the TARDIS Communicator and examine it. Ask TARDIS about Martin, Cassie, and entanglement. Explore inside, find Martin in the hallway, and ask about the mission. Take the cold suit when you encounter it.

02 — HIDDEN ROUTEOpen the secret passage

The Old Cellar hides a route into the cornfield. Two old paint cans provide the trigger; the passage is dark until you find a light source.

Hint 1
Inspect the shelves and the two cans in the Old Cellar. Try interacting with the cans together.
Hint 2
The opening leads into a dark passage. Follow the connected cornfield path toward the Cemetery.
Full Solution
TAP CANS
GO DOWN

Work through the cornfield route to the Cemetery and take the flashlight. Continue to the Clearing and take the Succulent, a treasure. Later, put the Succulent on the Quotient jet for an achievement bonus. Return through the passage with the flashlight and take the Spyglass.

03 — OXFORDRead the puzzle clue and locate the pendant

Oxford holds treasures and two clues that become important later: the password to Cassie's console and Lancelot's pendant.

Hint 1
Use the Spyglass on the completed jigsaw. In the VR meditation area, meditate and treat the resulting phrase as an anagram.
Hint 2
The puzzle code is 48151623. “THE OLDEST LOG” rearranges to the console password. Look behind the Sheldonian tapestry.
Full Solution

At the Clarendon Building, take and read the paper. In Duke Humfrey's Library, take the paperback. At the Sheldonian Theatre, look under the throne for the graduation program and read it. At the Turf Tavern, take the Pimm's. In Cassie's VR Lab, take the VR suit, enter the metaverse, and take the Yoga Mat in the meditation area. Meditate; “THE OLDEST LOG” is an anagram of THE LOST LODGE. Use BLINK to leave the metaverse.

With the Spyglass, examine the completed jigsaw to read code 48151623. At the Sheldonian, look behind the tapestry and take the pendant. Polish it with POLISH PENDANT — the pendant must be polished before it will work in the final sequence — then wear it for the time-travel sequence.

04 — SPACEReboot TARDIS

Reach the Spaceport and take the rocket to the TARDIS docking area. The jigsaw code opens the way in.

Hint 1
The rocket needs the “energy of an astronaut.” The keypad code is hidden in the jigsaw's fine print.
Hint 2
Drink Mountain Dew, enter the rocket, and use 48151623 at the TARDIS keypad.
Full Solution
FLY TO SPACEPORT
DRINK DEW
ENTER ROCKET
TYPE 48151623 ON KEYPAD
ENTER TARDIS

Go through the control center to the viewing area and open the protective shade. Reboot with CTRL-ALT-DELETE, then wait with Z until TARDIS is online. The reboot earns a 10-point achievement.

05 — WASHINGTON, D.C.Reveal the hidden laser clue

The National Cathedral chapel sequence reveals a laser. A reflective object from the Old Barn redirects its beam.

Hint 1
Speak with Jack at the Lincoln Memorial. In the cathedral chapel, the pew and a prayer matter. The laser needs a reflective target.
Hint 2
Take the Mirrorball from the Old Barn by standing on a chair. Hang it from the cathedral hook, then switch on the laser.
Full Solution
FLY TO WASHINGTON DC
ASK JACK ABOUT LASER
SIT ON PEW
PRAY
TURN ON LASER

At headquarters, stand on the chair in the Old Barn and take the Mirrorball. Return to the cathedral, hang the ball on the hook, and switch on the laser. It reveals a clock at about 1:07 and a lion-like creature. This sequence earns a 10-point achievement.

06 — AFRICARecover the Kyber crystal

Robert Clayton, also called the Lion, has the stolen crystal. The tranquilizer gun makes it possible to approach him safely.

Hint 1
The laser's lion clue points toward the Ngorongoro Crater. Bring the tranquilizer gun from the White House.
Hint 2
Use the gun on Clayton, then take the crystal.
Full Solution
FLY TO AFRICA
SHOOT CLAYTON WITH GUN
TAKE CRYSTAL

Recovering the crystal earns a 5-point achievement.

07 — CLEVELANDBuild the light saber and find the banknotes

At Nela Park / Building 336, a fluorescent tube provides housing for the crystal. Cleveland also holds the Zorkmid Banknotes and Millennium Falcon.

Hint 1
Look for a fluorescent tube at Building 336. Ask TARDIS about Cleveland's remaining treasure.
Hint 2
Put the Kyber crystal in the tube. Visit Edgewater Park and dig in the sand; the Falcon is the star ornament on the Lighting Institute's Christmas tree.
Full Solution
TAKE TUBE
PUT CRYSTAL IN TUBE

At Edgewater Park, use the shovel and keep digging until you uncover the Zorkmid Banknotes. At the Lighting Institute, examine the Christmas tree and take the Millennium Falcon from its top. Making the light saber earns a 10-point achievement.

09 — THE EXPERIMENTPower Cassie's quantum console

With the keycard, enter Cassie's lab in Oxford. The VR anagram gives the password; the light saber powers the console.

Hint 1
The password is the VR meditation anagram. Cassie's console needs an energy source.
Hint 2
Enter THE LOST LODGE on the keyboard, then place the light saber inside.
Full Solution
TYPE THE LOST LODGE ON KEYBOARD
OPEN CONSOLE
PUT LIGHT SABER IN CONSOLE
CLOSE CONSOLE
TURN ON CONSOLE

The binary display decodes to MARRY MARTIN. Cassie's experiment has sent a message through time.

10 — THE FINAL SEQUENCETouch Aslan and finish the mission

The last step connects the lion clue, the pendant, and the experiment's message. Find the Narnia Lamp in Oxford.

Hint 1
Examine the lion carving. If it glows, try making contact while wearing the pendant.
Hint 2
The lion is Aslan. Touch him to travel forward in time.
Full Solution
WEAR PENDANT
X ASLAN
TOUCH ASLAN

You arrive at the National Mall in 2050. The older couple on the lawn are Martin and Cassie. The experiment's message changed their earlier lives. Congratulations: you have won Quotient, the Game.
